# Illustration audit — Volume 1 (*Sokha’s Diary: The Road to the Bell*)

Checked against `Sokha_Art_Direction_and_Scene_Plan_EN.docx` and `Sokha_Illustration_Index.csv` (copies in `docs/source/`).
Register used by the build: `art/register.json` (generated by `art/build_register.py`). Numeric part:
`python3 build.py check --book sokha-road-to-the-bell`.

**Bottom line.** All 15 interior illustrations and both covers were supplied and are placed at their anchors in EN, FR and ES.
The nine half-page pictures (1448 × 1086 px) print at ≈ 322 ppi, which is print quality. The six full-page plates and both
cover images are **1024 × 1536 px, about 164–167 ppi at the page size; a printer wants 300**. The art-direction guide itself
asks for 2400 × 3600 px masters; what arrived are the generator’s first exports. Every file has the proportion the register asks
for (2:3 and 4:3), so nothing is cropped except about 0.06 in at the top and bottom of a plate to fill the bleed.

## 3. Continuity (art-direction rules)

Checked at review size (about 700 px wide) against the guide’s “Continuity” line for every picture:

* **Laterality.** In every front-facing picture the folded right trouser leg and the single crutch are on the viewer’s left
  (Maly’s right) and the sandal is on the viewer’s right. SK-I06 (overhead, head at the top) has the crutch on the viewer’s
  left, as the guide specifies. SK-I12 passes the notebook with the left hand; SK-I14 rings the bell with the left hand, crutch
  viewer-left, bell viewer-right. No second crutch, no prosthesis, no flipped artwork found.
* **Wood and metal; the ribbon.** Wooden crutch with the pink ribbon in I01, I03, I05, I06 (with the blue cover), I07 (cover
  crooked, ribbon still tied); wooden and bare in I08 and I09; metal with the ribbon on the table in I10; metal with the ribbon
  tied in I11, I12, I13, I14, I15.
* **Objects and timing.** Blue pen not yet in the story in I01; the little bicycle bell held by Lina in I04, clamped but not
  rung in I11, rung in I14; the rope is red and white and the right-hand branch is the closed one in I05; no explosive is shown
  anywhere; yellow shirt (I13, I14) and teal shirt (I11) as the guide asks.
* **Agency.** Nobody is carried in any picture, nobody crosses a closed route, no miraculous recovery.

Small points worth a glance (none contradicts the brief):

1. **SK-I02:** a second football lies at the foot of the tree, in addition to the one in the tall grass.
2. **SK-I07:** a closed notebook lies on the ground next to the crutch; the chapter does not mention it at that moment.
3. **SK-I08:** Sokha is the one holding the blue pen in this picture; in the text he has just handed it to Maly.
4. **SK-I09:** the map on the mat is an unlabeled route sketch. The guide says labels are added by the designer; none were
   added, because legible lettering over the picture would be invented content. The Book of Steps is described in the text.
5. **Two rendering looks.** A few pictures (I10, I12, I14) have a smoother, cleaner finish than the softer
   gouache-and-pencil look of I01, I03, I09. Faces, clothes and props stay recognisable throughout (Sokha: rust T-shirt, charcoal
   shorts; Maly: teal blouse, navy trousers, low ponytail; Mae: indigo blouse, checked krama; Lina: cream blouse, green trousers;
   Sreyneang: pale blue shirt). One regeneration pass with a single style prompt would unify the set and fix the resolution.

## 4. Where pictures sit in the story

Anchors are one sentence of the paragraph named in the art-direction guide, per language; `python3 build.py check` fails if an
edit breaks one. The three manuscripts do not have the same paragraph count in every chapter, so each picture lists the
paragraph that carries it in EN, FR and ES. Inline pictures follow their anchor paragraph exactly. Full-page plates may move to
the end of the page that holds their anchor (never before it) so that no text page before a plate is left almost empty; two
slots carry extra rules in the register:

* `stop`: the plate should not pass this paragraph (SK-I07 stays before Mae appears; SK-I12 before Sokha seals the bag, unless
  that would leave the page mostly empty);
* `early`: the plate may stand at the page turn just before its anchor paragraph when that paragraph would start a page
  (SK-I10: the clinic picture then precedes “What do you think?”, which is where the guide wants it, before the outdoor trial).
