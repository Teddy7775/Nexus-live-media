# Illustration audit — Volume 2 (*The Drum and the Ball: Lamine’s Journal*)

Checked against `Illustration_Register.csv`, `Art_Direction_EN.txt` and `production_manifest.json` (copies in `docs/source/`).
Register used by the build: `art/register.json` (generated by `art/build_register.py`). Numeric part:
`python3 build.py check --book lamine-drum-and-ball`.

**Bottom line.** All 15 interior illustrations and both covers arrived, with their IDs, and are placed at their anchors in EN, FR
and ES. Unlike the previous volume, **the interior pictures are print resolution**: the fourteen landscape pictures print at
about 341–372 ppi and the portrait one (DB-I10) at about 299 ppi. The two **cover images are 1024 × 1536 px, about 164–167 ppi
at the cover size; a printer wants 300**. The art-direction file says so itself (“obtain a higher-resolution production master
or approve a smaller trim before press”).

How the files were identified. Pictures sent earlier in the conversation, before the coded set, were set aside at the author’s
instruction. The coded set arrived in four messages in register order; each file was then compared with its scene in the
register (who is in it, what they hold, what they wear, the light) and every one matches its ID. Nothing was cropped, upscaled or
repainted.

* **Placement rules followed.** Each picture goes immediately after its anchor paragraph, or at the next page end without
  preceding the narrated event. The art direction asks for generous inset plates, images kept whole, no crop of hands, laces,
  notebook or ball, and the portrait DB-I10 as a full-page inset: that is why DB-I10 is a new slot kind, `inset` (centred on a page
  of its own with paper all round, no bleed, no crop), and why no picture of this volume is a full-bleed plate.
* **DB-I10.** It follows the conversation (“Then what can I hold on to?” … “Grownups go very quiet exactly when their answers might
  actually be useful.”) and stays before “I came in here to write.” The page before it is about 69 % full, just under the usual 70 %
  threshold (the layout pass measures 60 % at the top of the last line), so the register carries `min_fill` (0.58; 0.50 in
  Spanish, where the page before the picture is about 58 % full) for this one picture. Without it the picture would float past “I can promise three things:”, away from the conversation it illustrates.
* **No goal picture before its reveal.** DB-I11 (Lamine passes to Moussa) follows “Then I pushed the ball between Cheikh and
  Malick.” and DB-I12 follows “The net moved.”; neither image shows the final score. DB-I13 (the handover) sits in the next-morning
  chapter, not at the whistle.
* ID labels appear nowhere in the reading edition.
* To reach 300 ppi on the covers, ask the generator for **1875 × 2775 px or more** (2400 × 3600 is better), save the original as
  `art/final/DB-C01_v01.png` and `art/final/DB-C04_v01.png`, and run `python3 build.py print --book lamine-drum-and-ball --final`.
  `final/` wins over `source/`; the final build refuses anything below 250 ppi.

## 2. Covers

* **DB-C01** is the symbolic father-and-son composition the art direction asks to keep (sunset, baobab, river, the old ball,
  Baba’s goblet drum and beads). The title is set in ivory serif over a dark gradient on the indigo sky; the series line and the
  subtitle are smaller, as the direction suggests; the author line is a proof placeholder.
* **DB-C04** is a quiet still life (old ball, closed journal, two red laces) against a plain wall. The copy sits on a paper label
  over the wall because black text on the orange wall was not legible; the publisher line and the barcode area are at lower right,
  clear of the still life. No barcode, ISBN or blurb is embedded in the art.
* The spine is computed from each interior’s real page count (`spine_in = pages × 0.0025`, cream paper) and must be confirmed with
  the printer’s own template.

## 3. Continuity checks

Looked at in the files themselves (contact sheets at about 560 px per picture, then the placed pages), against the “Scene mechanics”
and “Character continuity” sections of the art direction. These were checked and hold:

* DB-I01 black plastic peels to gray cloth, plain laces on Lamine’s shoes (the red laces are not given until chapter 4); DB-I02
  Lamine carries the yellow basin and Awa the small jug; DB-I03 sand-coloured boubou for Baba, oversized boubou for Lamine, the
  family-name book (not the black journal) open on his lap; DB-I04 two loose red laces, reconciliation partial (Lamine looks away);
  DB-I05 Fatou reads the rules, no opposing team yet, Cheikh’s white-and-blue ball distinct from the patched ball.
* DB-I07 morning light and the kingfisher with a fish in its beak, Baba not skipping stones; DB-I08 Lamine holds the closed black
  journal, Baba’s hands are open, nobody threatens; DB-I09 Diatta, gloved, handles the sharp debris, the wheelbarrow is stable and
  the cleaned hole is being filled by shovel (the dossier’s second version); DB-I11 Lamine passes to Moussa and does not score;
  DB-I12 Idrissa in olive green, a borrowed small goal with a net; DB-I13 the courtyard, the next morning; DB-I14 Baba listens
  with his hands on his knees and does not touch the journal; DB-I15 the old black-plastic ball and Baba’s drum at home, none at
  the match.
* Two balls are never confused: the old ball is black plastic and tape; Cheikh’s training ball is white and blue; the match ball
  (DB-I11, DB-I12) is off-white with dark panels.

Small points, none of which stops the build. They are for the author or the artist to decide:

1. **Red sandal straps.** Lamine’s and Awa’s sandals carry red straps in DB-I02, Lamine’s in DB-I15 and on the front cover. The
   dossier makes the dark red laces the small friendship motif and says never to substitute red sandal straps for them. The laces
   are never replaced, but red straps before the laces exist (DB-I02, 7 February) and in the epilogue dilute the motif.
2. **Awa’s dress.** Coral in DB-I01, DB-I06 and DB-I13 as the brief says; a deeper brick red in DB-I02 and a pinker tone in DB-I15.
3. **The old ball.** Black plastic strips, tape and gray cloth in DB-I01, DB-I15 and both covers; in DB-I05 the ball at Lamine’s
   feet reads as a darker patched ball with a different texture.
4. **DB-I10 is a different hand.** It is the retained supplied image: smoother and more painterly than the inked, flatter finish of
   the other pictures, and lit at dusk like DB-I14 and DB-I15. It works as the one quiet “rest” of the book; it is also the picture
   that most shows that the set comes from two sources.
5. **Geography.** The art direction itself says the architecture and landscape are story illustrations, not documentary evidence
   of Bambali.

## 4. Not part of the book

The dossier also holds the ten character-sheet prompts (CS-01 to CS-10), nineteen source images (SRC-01 to SRC-19: kept, corrected
or discarded) and reference drafts; none of them is an interior image and none is placed. The previous-version files
(`DB-I09-rejected-v1`, `DB-I12-before-correction`) are not used: the book uses the final files named in the register.
