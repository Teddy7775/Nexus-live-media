# Illustration audit — Volume 2 (*Anastasiya’s Echo: Katya’s Notebook*)

Checked against `Anastasiya_Art_Direction_EN.docx`, `Anastasiya_Illustration_Dossier_EN_Source.docx`
and `Anastasiya_Illustration_Index_ID.csv` (copies in `docs/source/`). Register used by the build:
`art/register.json` (generated by `art/build_register.py`). Numeric part: `python3 build.py check --book anastasiya-echo`.

**Bottom line.** All 15 interior illustrations and both covers were supplied and are placed at their anchors in
EN, FR and ES. They are good enough for proofs, the website and the ebooks. They are **not print masters**:
five full-page plates and both cover images are 896 × 1200 px (≈ 143–146 ppi at print size, a printer wants
300), and nine have the wrong proportion for their slot. **Four pictures (I04, I11, I13, I14) were regenerated after
the first review**; most of the continuity problems found then are fixed (section 3), two are not.

* The files were delivered inside the chat, i.e. already downscaled. Nothing in the build can add detail,
  and nothing was upscaled.
* The guide asks for **1800 × 2700 px** (portrait) and **1800 × 1350 px** (half page) at minimum; the bleed
  version is 1875 × 2775 px. Put the generator’s original PNG/TIFF exports into `art/final/` with the same
  names (`AE-I01_v01.png` …) and run `python3 build.py print --book anastasiya-echo --final`.
  `final/` wins over `source/`.
* **Proportion.** The nine full-page portraits are 3:4 (0.747), the register asks for 2:3 (0.667). On a full-bleed
  page they are scaled to the page height and about 9 % of the width is cropped (≈ 0.65 in in total, split left and
  right). Faces and key objects stay inside the crop in all nine, but anything near the left or right edge
  (I01 backpack and gate, I08 straw, I15 gate post) is trimmed. Regenerating at 2:3 removes the crop.
* **Two alternates** were supplied as 2000 × 2000 squares: `AE-I10_v01_ALT.jpg` and `AE-I12_v01_ALT.jpg`. I kept
  the portrait versions as the primary because the register calls for full-page plates. The squares are sharper
  (≈ 444 ppi inline) but would be inline pictures, not plates. Tell me if you prefer them; it is a one-line change.

## 2. Covers

* **AE-C01** was composed with the top ~28 % left as quiet paper. The cover builder keeps that paper for the series
  name, title and descriptor, extends it upward by mirroring the paper’s own edge so it reaches the bleed, and puts
  the author name on the thin paper strip at the bottom. Nothing was redrawn.
* **AE-C04** carries a pale rectangle at the bottom right for the barcode (about 1.75 × 1.1 in). Printers add their
  own white 2 × 1.2 in box there; both are fine. The scan-edge strip on the left (40 px) is cropped.
* Spine: slate blue from the palette, no scene added, title and author when the width allows (all six wraps do).

  ids never appear inside an illustration, so the build paints those letters out (only the lettering pixels are repainted from the

**Still open**

2. **AE-I09 and AE-C04: the wooden bird.** The brief requires the left wing to be visibly shorter than the right; at this

## 4. Where pictures sit in the story

Anchors are one sentence of the paragraph named in the index, per language; `python3 build.py check` fails if an
edit breaks one. Inline pictures follow their anchor paragraph exactly. Full-page plates may move to the end of
the page that holds their anchor (never before it) so no text page before a picture is left almost empty; a plate
stays before the next notebook page unless that would leave a page mostly blank.
